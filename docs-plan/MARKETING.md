## 0. Ground rules (read first)

1. **Do not promote a site that gives wrong answers.** PLAN.md 3.4 already gates growth behind reliability. Marketing starts only when this gate is met: *10 consecutive weekdays with a successful run before 08:30 and fresh coverage ≥ 70%* (PLAN P-1, P-3). One stale Monday after a poster goes up costs more trust than the poster earns.
2. **Measure from the first scan.** Cookieless analytics (PLAN U-1) and `?src=` tags (6.2 below) must be live before any QR code is printed.
3. **Neutrality is the product.** "Dagens tips" picks are editorial and never paid. Anything paid (R1, R2) is labelled `Annons` (marknadsföringslagen § 9, see PLAN 3.6).
4. **Permission before presence.** Every poster, table tent and restaurant mention is placed with the owner's or building's permission. Every restaurant gets the menu-permission email from PLAN 3.6 before it is promoted.
5. **Jay publishes and sends.** The assistant researches, drafts and prepares. Nothing is posted, submitted or emailed without Jay's explicit approval of that exact item (Part 4).

## 1. Positioning, name, voice and audiences

### 1.1 Positioning

**For** people who work, study or visit at Lindholmen
**who** have to decide where to eat between roughly 11:00 and 13:00 **[ASSUMPTION]**,
**Lindholmen Lunch** is **the one page with every nearby lunch menu for today**,
**that** answers "var äter vi idag?" in a few seconds on a phone, in Swedish or English, with no app, no login and no tracking cookies.
**Unlike** restaurant Instagram accounts, scattered websites or national lunch directories, it is **local, fast, neutral and designed** (made by a Lindholmen-area designer, PLAN R8).

Three proof points to repeat everywhere:

1. **All of Lindholmen on one page**: 15 restaurants today, updated every weekday morning.
2. **Seconds, not minutes**: filters per dish (vego, fisk, kyckling…), "Välj åt mig", "Pick for me" (PLAN U-7, U-9).
3. **Respectful**: free, no app, no cookies, no ads in your face (PLAN U-1).

### 1.2 Name

Keep **Lindholmen Lunch** and the domain **lindholmenlunch.nu**. The name is literal and searchable, and the domain already has history. Changing it would throw away SEO and printed QR codes. Use the PLAN 2.4 C1 wordmark `lindholmen ◦ lunch` everywhere.

Social handles to check and reserve in Days 1–14 (pick the first one available on all of Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, then use it everywhere):

1. `@lindholmenlunch`
2. `@lindholmen.lunch`
3. `@lunchlindholmen`

The `.nu` ending means "now" in Swedish. Use that in taglines: it makes the URL itself the call to action.

### 1.4 Voice

The voice follows the Jay Digital Studio tone already measured in PLAN 2.1: plain, short, confident, a little dry. It is a helpful neighbour, not a hype account.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences. Headings end with a full stop: "Lunch idag." | Exclamation marks, "MEGA", "BÄSTA", "🔥🔥🔥" |
| Concrete: dish, price, place. "Lax med dillsås, 129 kr, Bistrot." | Vague superlatives: "amazing food scene" |
| One emoji at most, and only if it adds meaning (🐟 for fish) | Emoji as decoration |
| Neutral about restaurants. Picks are explained ("veg + under 120 kr") | Trashing a restaurant, or ranking them by taste |
| Swedish first for locals, English always available | Swenglish mixes in one sentence ("Kolla in dagens lunch deals") |
| Admit gaps: "6 menyer saknas idag, vi jobbar på det." | Pretending coverage is complete |

**Language rule:** Swedish and English are separate posts or separate lines, never mixed in one sentence. On Instagram and TikTok, use Swedish on-screen text with an English line in the caption. On LinkedIn, Jay posts in the language of his own network (default Swedish, English for international-team posts). Lindholmen has many international employees and students **[ASSUMPTION]**, so English should never be an afterthought.

Word bank (SV / EN): dagens lunch / today's lunch · lunchmeny / lunch menu · vego / veggie · Välj åt mig / Pick for me · var äter vi? / where are we eating? · hela Lindholmen / all of Lindholmen · uppdaterad varje vardag / updated every weekday.

### 2.2 Tactic playbooks

#### T-QR · QR posters and table tents (Rank 1)

- **Where:** restaurant tables and counters (with owner OK), office lunch rooms and kitchens (via office managers), lift lobbies and notice boards (via building reception or LSP), campus notice boards (via student union or campus service desk). **[VERIFY each building's posting rules.]**
- **What:** A4/A3 poster, A6 folded table tent, 50 mm round sticker for coffee machines and microwaves (where the microwave queue happens, students and matlåda people are captive for 2 minutes).
- **Copy:** `Var äter vi idag?` / `Where are we eating today?` + `lindholmenlunch.nu` + QR + one line: `Alla lunchmenyer på Lindholmen. Uppdateras varje vardag.`
- **Tracking:** a unique QR per location: `https://lindholmenlunch.nu/?src=qr-<place>` (6.2). Keep a placement log (sheet tab `Placements`: location, permission given by, date up, date checked, scans per week).
- **Rule:** never put a poster up without permission, never cover others' notices, re-check every 4 weeks and remove damaged ones.
- **Generate QR codes locally** (e.g. the `qrcode` Python package or Figma plugin), not via a third-party QR shortener that may expire or track. The current `flyer.html` uses `api.qrserver.com` at render time; for print, generate static vector QR files once.

#### T-REST · Restaurant partnerships (Rank 2)

The pitch is "You're already listed, free. Let's make it more useful for you."

1. **Walk in** Mon–Thu after 14:00 with a printed one-pager (asset A12) and the table tent. Ask: (a) permission to show the menu (PLAN 3.6), (b) a better menu source (a URL, a Google Sheet, or the self-serve form when R1 ships), (c) table tents on tables, (d) a "Vi finns på lindholmenlunch.nu" window sticker, (e) a mention or story share when we feature them.
2. **Give first:** each partner gets a feature post in the content calendar (Part 3), and once analytics is live, a monthly email with their outbound clicks ("23 personer klickade vidare till er i oktober" **— example format, not data**). That report is the seed of the paid R1 tier.
3. **Ask restaurants to fix their own Google Business Profile** with a "Meny" link (see T-GBP). It helps them and strengthens the local web around the site.
4. **Never** imply endorsement they didn't give, use their logo or photos without permission, or rank them by quality.

#### T-SLACK · Slack/Teams communities and office champions (Rank 3)

- **Before R4 exists:** encourage manual sharing. A "Kopiera länk för lunchtråden" / "Copy link for the lunch thread" share action is a small product ask (6.4). Office champions paste the link in `#lunch` channels.
- **R4 free pilot (PLAN 3.3, days 31–60):** a Slack incoming webhook posting at 10:45. Recruit champions from Jay's network and LinkedIn replies, never by cold-joining workspaces.
- **Community Slacks/Discords** Jay is legitimately a member of (local tech, design, startup communities **[VERIFY which exist and their self-promotion rules]**): one launch post in the channel meant for it (`#show-and-tell`, `#lokalt`), following each community's rules. No repeated posting, no DMs to members.
- **Teams:** Teams usually means larger corporates with stricter IT. Offer the `/screen/` view (R3) or a plain link for the intranet instead of a bot until there's demand.

#### T-HOOK · Daily "Dagens tips" / "Today's pick" habit (Rank 4)

A single, fast, repeatable daily touchpoint at the decision time.

- **What:** one dish per weekday, chosen by a transparent rule that rotates, e.g. Mon "Veckans start: vego", Tue "Under 120 kr", Wed "Fisk", Thu "Något nytt", Fri "Fredagsfavorit". This spreads attention across restaurants (neutral) and gives each day a recognisable format.
- **Where:** Instagram story at ~10:30 (template A7), the Slack post (R4), and optionally a small `Dagens tips` badge on the site (product ask; it must not be confused with `Annons`).
- **Cost of the habit:** 5 minutes per day once templated. The assistant prepares the draft from the day's menu by 09:30 (Part 4, B4); Jay approves with one tap and posts.
- **Rule:** picks are never paid. If R1 "Utvald" exists later, it is a separate, labelled slot.
- **Fallback:** if Jay can't keep a daily cadence, run it Mon/Wed/Fri. Consistency matters more than frequency.

#### T-SEO · Local SEO and Google (Rank 5)

Target queries (validate volumes in Search Console after 4 weeks; any volume estimate now is guesswork):

- SV: `lunch lindholmen`, `dagens lunch lindholmen`, `lunch lindholmen idag`, `lunchmeny lindholmen`, `lunch hisingen`, `lunch lindholmspiren` **[VERIFY local place names people use]**, `vegetarisk lunch lindholmen`, `<restaurant name> lunch`.
- EN: `lunch lindholmen`, `lunch near lindholmen science park`, `lunch near chalmers lindholmen`.

Actions (most are product asks for the implementer; marked with PLAN IDs where they already exist):

1. **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools**: Jay verifies the domain (DNS TXT) and submits `sitemap.xml`. Free, and it gives the only reliable ranking data (do not scrape Google results, see 4.3).
2. **On-page**: `<title>` like `Lunch Lindholmen idag – alla menyer | Lindholmen Lunch`, a meta description using T2, a visible H1 for crawlers (PLAN U-4 keeps a visually hidden H1), canonical and OG tags (PLAN U-12).
3. **Restaurant name pages**: people search "`{restaurant} lunch`". A small per-restaurant page or anchor (`/r/bistrot/`) with today's menu and a link to the restaurant is a strong long-tail win. This is a product ask (6.4), and it only makes sense with restaurant permission (PLAN 3.6).
4. **Structured data**: `WebSite` and `ItemList` of restaurants linking to their sites. Don't mark the aggregator up as a `Restaurant` or `LocalBusiness`; it isn't one.
5. **Backlinks that are earned and local**: LSP pages, student union pages, restaurant sites ("Se dagens meny på lindholmenlunch.nu"), office intranets (not indexed but drive traffic), event pages, jaydigitalstudio.com case study (R8), the open-source GitHub README. No link buying, no link exchanges with unrelated sites.
6. **Freshness**: `lastmod` in the sitemap from the generator (PLAN U-12).

#### T-GBP · Google Business Profile (Rank 14), eligibility warning

**lindholmenlunch.nu is probably not eligible for its own Google Business Profile.** Google's guidelines require a business to make in-person contact with customers at a physical location or within a service area; online-only services are not eligible. Creating a listing with a made-up or virtual address, or for a business that doesn't meet customers in person, breaks the guidelines and risks suspension. **Excluded.** **[VERIFY current guidelines before deciding.]**

What to do instead:

- If **Jay Digital Studio AB** already has (or qualifies for) a profile, keep it accurate and mention Lindholmen Lunch as a project in its posts or description. That supports R8.
- Help **restaurants** keep *their* profiles accurate (hours, website, a menu link, lunch photos). The assistant can audit public profile info and draft a friendly note to each restaurant (Part 4, B7). Jay edits a restaurant's profile **only** if the owner grants manager access and asks for help.
- Don't use "Suggest an edit" on other businesses' profiles to insert links to lindholmenlunch.nu.

## 4. Browser automation playbook

Jay's AI assistant drives a real browser signed in to Jay's accounts. These rules apply to every task:

### 4.0 Operating rules

1. **Draft, don't publish.** The assistant never posts, sends, submits, comments, reacts, follows, connects or buys. The final click is Jay's, or the assistant clicks only after Jay approves that exact item in writing in the same session ("Approve: post #3 as shown").
2. **Human pace, public pages only.** Read pages one at a time as a person would. No bulk scraping, no bypassing logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or `robots.txt` disallows, no hidden accounts.
3. **Data minimisation (GDPR).** Collect company-level data by default. Collect a named person's work contact only if it is published by the company or person for business contact, and only when needed for a specific outreach. Record the source URL and date for every row.
4. **One source of truth:** a Google Sheet `LL Growth` (stored in Jay's Google account) with tabs `KPIs`, `Contacts`, `Do-not-contact`, `Content`, `Placements`, `Directories`, `Mentions`, `Competitors`, `SEO`, `Press`, `Log`.
5. **Log everything:** each run appends a line to the sheet's `Log` tab: task, date, pages visited (count), what was produced, what awaits approval.

### 4.4 GDPR notes for B1–B3, B9 (not legal advice)

- **Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) for one-to-one B2B outreach to published business contacts about a relevant, free local service. Jay writes a one-page legitimate interest assessment once (purpose, necessity, balancing) and keeps it with the sheet.
- **Transparency:** the first email says who Jay is, where the contact came from, why, and how to opt out, with a link to the privacy page (GDPR art. 14). The PLAN U-1 privacy page rewrite should get a short "Kontakt med företag och restauranger" section (product ask).
- **Minimisation:** company-level data by default. For people: name, role, business email or phone, source URL, date. Nothing else. No personal social profiles, no private numbers, no special-category data.
- **Retention:** delete person-level rows with no reply after 6 months, and any row on request. Keep only the address in `Do-not-contact` (needed to honour the objection).
- **Opt-out:** honoured immediately and permanently.
- **Storage:** Jay's Google account, sharing restricted to Jay. No export to third-party tools without a data processing agreement.
- **Email to companies vs people:** marknadsföringslagen's opt-in rule (§ 19) targets natural persons; email to a company's generic address (info@, reception@) carries less risk. A named employee's work address is personal data under GDPR either way, so the legitimate-interest and transparency steps above always apply. **[VERIFY with IMY guidance if in doubt.]**

3. **Implementation asks (for the implementer, 6.4):** read `src` on load, send it as a custom property on the pageview, then remove it from the address bar with `history.replaceState` so shared links don't inherit the original source.
